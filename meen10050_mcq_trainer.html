SSSF answer skeleton
SSSF = steady-state steady-flow. Steady-state: no accumulation of mass or energy in the control volume. Steady-flow: inlet/outlet conditions constant with time. Qdot_in + Σ m_dot_in(h + V²/2 + gz) = Wdot_out + Σ m_dot_out(h + V²/2 + gz) 1-in, 1-out: Qdot_in − Wdot_out = m_dot[(h2−h1)+(V2²−V1²)/2+g(z2−z1)] h = u + Pv, so flow work is already inside enthalpy.

Heat transfer formulas
Conduction: Qdot = kAΔT/L Rcond = L/(kA) Convection: Qdot = hA(Ts−T∞) Rconv = 1/(hA) Radiation: Qdot = εσA(Ts⁴−Tsur⁴) temperatures in K Series network: Qdot = ΔT / Rtotal Overall U: Qdot = UAΔT
Ideal gas / closed systems / processes
PV = mRT m = PV/(RT) R = Ru/M Δu = cvΔT Δh = cpΔT Constant volume: W = 0 Constant pressure: W = P(V2−V1) = mR(T2−T1) Isothermal: W = P1V1 ln(V2/V1) Polytropic: PV^n = const; P2=P1(V1/V2)^n; W=(P2V2−P1V1)/(1−n)
